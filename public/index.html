Complaint process

How a member concern is handled

  1. Receive

    • Log the concern with dates and location.
    • Record the effect on residents.
    • Attach relevant evidence.

  2. Review

    • Confirm the applicable service or rule.
    • Arrange inspection where needed.
    • Keep allegations separate from findings.

  3. Respond

    • Record the committee’s decision.
    • Explain the outcome and next step.
    • Keep a dated communication record.

  4. Follow up

    • Request the member’s feedback.
    • Confirm completed action.
    • Keep unresolved points open.

Source: Society Work — Operating Review, slide 6.
